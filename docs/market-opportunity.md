# Market opportunity — illustrative planning scenarios

**Internal planning only.** Not on the homepage or any merchant dashboard. If ever
published, only on a secondary page captioned "Illustrative planning estimate", with the
assumptions below and external figures kept separate.

Figures from the redesign brief v3.0 (20 Nov 2026). They are modelling assumptions, not
market research, not actual users, revenue or traction, and not a forecast. All use the
Starter list price of ₦10,000/month = ₦120,000/year.

| Scenario | Assumption | × annual list price | Annual gross software-spend opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TAM | 200,000 addressable beauty retail and services businesses across selected African markets | ₦120,000 | ₦24 billion |
| SAM | 40,000 eligible, reachable Nigerian beauty businesses | ₦120,000 | ₦4.8 billion |
| SOM (target scenario) | 400 subscribed businesses | ₦120,000 | ₦48 million annualised at list |

At the annual-prepaid Starter price (₦96,000, 20% off), 400 businesses would be ₦38.4 million
in annual prepaid billings before churn, taxes or adjustments. Actual value depends on plan
and discount mix.

To validate: merchant counts (formal and informal), reachability by channel and geography,
willingness to pay, and plan mix. No source verifies the number of beauty merchants or any
GlowHub adoption.

## External context (different segments — not GlowHub's addressable revenue)

Cited in the brief; check each source before publishing anything from it.

- Euromonitor International (press release, Dec 2025): Middle East and Africa beauty and
  personal care described as a US$44 billion market in 2025.
- MarkNtel Advisors (Nigeria beauty and personal care market study): Nigeria estimated at
  US$1.17 billion in 2025.

These measure consumer beauty spending, not B2B beauty software, and use different
geographies and methods.
