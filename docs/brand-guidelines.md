# Brand guidelines (prototype)

Status: proposal for the prototype, pending brand approval.

## Positioning
- Proposition: **Beautiful businesses. Intelligent commerce.**
- Secondary line: The AI-powered operating system for Africa's beauty businesses.
- Tagline: BEAUTY MEETS INTELLIGENCE.
- Tone: premium, contemporary, African, elegant and trustworthy. Consumer pages are
  editorial and warm; merchant and admin interfaces are restrained and operational.

## Logo
All files are in `public/brand/` and generated by `npm run brand:generate` from
Cormorant Garamond 600 and Manrope 600 (both SIL Open Font Licence 1.1). Glyphs are
converted to vector outlines with tuned kerning; the period is enlarged 25% so the rose
accent survives small sizes. Do not hand-edit generated files.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `glowhub-wordmark.svg` | Primary: plum letters, rose period, light backgrounds |
| `glowhub-wordmark-reverse.svg` | Cream letters, rose period, on plum |
| `glowhub-wordmark-mono.svg` / `-mono-white.svg` | Single-colour reproduction |
| `glowhub-ai-extended.svg` (+ `-reverse`) | "glowhub ai" product name |
| `glowhub-lockup.svg` (+ `-reverse`) | Wordmark with tagline |
| `glowhub-compact.svg` | "g." without tile, tight spaces |
| `glowhub-monogram-rose.svg` / `-plum.svg` | "g." on a rounded tile |
| `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-*.png` | Browser and PWA icons |

Rules:
- Keep clear space of at least the height of the "o" around the wordmark.
- Wordmark minimum height 20px; below that use the monogram.
- Don't recolour beyond the supplied variants, stretch, add effects or place on busy
  photography without a solid panel.
- No sparkles, faces or flowers as the main mark.

## Photography direction
Premium African beauty editorial: diverse adult skin tones, natural skin texture,
confident women of slender and curvy body types, contemporary natural hairstyles,
cream/blush/sage studio light. Authentic beauty entrepreneurs in stores, salons and
studios. Product imagery: fictional creams, moisturisers, serums, cleanser, sunscreen and
body care on neutral backgrounds.

Requirements: licensed or original assets only, with releases; no watermarks, hotlinking
or unlicensed search images; no excessive retouching, skin-lightening ideals or
before/after deception; never imply models are customers. Store optimised WebP/AVIF under
`public/images/{hero,beauty-editorial,skin-scan,beauty-businesses,skincare-products,product-previews,consultation,storefronts}`
and record each asset's source and licence in `docs/image-credits.md` (created in M2).
