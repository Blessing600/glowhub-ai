# Brand guidelines — Stone & Plum

Adopted 20 Nov 2026 (redesign brief v3.0), replacing Emerald Atelier. Mood: an
aspirational, premium African beauty brand that happens to be intelligent — warm stone and
ivory neutrals, editorial serif type, restrained colour.

## Positioning
- GlowHub AI is beauty and skincare intelligence for shoppers and beauty businesses
  (skincare retailers, beauty brands, salons, spas, distributors). Not a marketplace with a
  chatbot attached, and not fashion or tailoring.
- Homepage line: **Beauty, beautifully understood.** Supporting: *Skincare that feels made
  for you.* Business line: **Beauty businesses, beautifully run.**
- Character: confident, feminine, sophisticated, warm, modern, authentically African.
- The site is live. Never present it as pre-launch, a waitlist, a demo, a prototype or a
  proposed concept.

## Colour
Tokens: `src/config/brand.ts`, mirrored in `src/app/globals.css`; `contrast.test.ts` checks
every approved pairing against WCAG 2.2 AA. Neutrals generously; one accent at a time.

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ivory | #FFF7EF | Page canvas |
| stone | #DDD3C6 | Hero and editorial panels |
| sand | #F3EADF | Light warm panels, selected states |
| line | #E4D9CC | Borders and rules |
| plum | #342038 | Primary action, dark sections, headings |
| plum-600 | #4B2F51 | Primary hover |
| espresso / ink | #302420 | Body text, footer |
| taupe | #5E5049 | Secondary text |
| rose (hibiscus) | #B9527C | Accent: rules, icons, focus ring, display text ≥24px |
| rose-700 | #94395F | Accent text at normal size |
| gold (antique) | #C59B5D | Detail; as text only on plum/espresso |
| terracotta | #B96546 | Occasional display accent ≥24px |

Never put white or ivory small text on rose or terracotta. Status is never shown by colour alone.

## Typography
- **Playfair Display** — hero and selected large editorial headings, regular weight with
  italic emphasis. Adopted 19 Nov 2026 for the premium redesign.
- **Manrope** — body, CTAs, menus, forms, tables, dashboards and eyebrows.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — system labels inside product interfaces only.
Loaded with `next/font/google` as CSS variables (`--font-playfair`, `--font-manrope`,
`--font-plex-mono`). The logo remains drawn from Bodoni Moda (see below).

## Logo
Generated by `npm run brand:generate` (Bodoni Moda 500 + Manrope 600, SIL OFL 1.1) as vector
outlines into `public/brand/`. Do not hand-edit.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `glowhub-wordmark.svg` | Emerald "glowhub", copper diamond, "AI" — light backgrounds |
| `glowhub-wordmark-reverse.svg` | Ivory letters on emerald/forest |
| `glowhub-wordmark-mono(-white).svg` | Single-colour reproduction |
| `glowhub-monogram.svg` | "g" with copper diamond on an emerald tile |
| `glowhub-compact.svg` | Monogram without tile |
| `favicon.*`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-*.png` | Browser and PWA icons |

Minimum wordmark height 20px; below that use the monogram. No trademark claim is made.

## Visual system
Editorial asymmetry, wide photographic panels, fine rules, generous negative space.
Avoid rows of identical cards, gradients, glassmorphism, heavy shadows, sparkles, emoji,
robot/chatbot art and excessive badges. Motion only where it clarifies; respect
`prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Photography
Empowered African women in authentic beauty-business settings, diverse skin tones, ages,
hair and bodies; alternate people and product close-ups. No skin-tone hierarchy, fake
transformations, heavy retouching or implying models are customers or Glow Scan users.
Licensed or commissioned assets only, recorded in `docs/photo-credits.md`.
